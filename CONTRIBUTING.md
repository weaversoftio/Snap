# 🤝 Contributing to Snap

Thank you for your interest in contributing to **Snap** — the Kubernetes Pod Checkpoint and Restore Operator developed by **[Weaversoft.io](https://weaversoft.io)**.

We welcome contributions from the community to help improve Snap and make container checkpointing and restoration more reliable and efficient across Kubernetes environments.

---

## 🧱 Project Overview

Snap automates container checkpointing, migration, and restore processes within Kubernetes and OpenShift clusters.

If you’d like to contribute code, documentation, or ideas — you’re in the right place!

---
